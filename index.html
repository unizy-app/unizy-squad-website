About UniZy

What is UniZy?

UniZy is a marketplace app just for university students, built by LSE alumni. Students buy and sell items and services with each other, and every user is verified with a university email.

What can you find on it?

  • Value FindsNew and second-hand items, such as electronics, books, furniture and clothing.
  • Study MateTutoring and academic help across subjects, courses and modules.
  • Homemade MealsHomemade food, with ingredient, diet and allergen information.
  • Home SearchRooms, sublets and tenancy replacements.
  • Tidy UpSmall household help, such as cleaning, laundry and organising.

How is it different from competitors?

  • It’s only for students. Everyone verifies a university email, so users are buying from and selling to other students.
  • It’s everything students need in one place. Alongside second-hand items, students can find tutoring, homemade food, household help and rooms, all in one app.
  • It’s built around universities. Every listing shows the seller’s university, and students can filter listings by university as well as distance. Buying from someone at the same university makes it easy to agree a common spot to meet, like the campus or library.

Buying and selling

How does paying work, and why is it safer?

  1. The buyer pays in the app, and UniZy holds the money.
  2. A six-digit transaction verification code appears in the buyer’s Transactions tab.
  3. Once the buyer has the item or service, they give the code to the seller.
  4. The seller enters it in My Listings, and the payment is released.

This protects both sides. The seller knows the money is there before handing anything over, and the buyer’s money only reaches the seller once they have what they paid for. Until the code is shared, the buyer can cancel and nothing is paid. Once the code is used, the sale is final, so buyers should only share it when they’re happy.

Home Search works differently. The seller pays a fee to list, there’s no payment in the app, and both sides arrange everything through chat.

If something goes wrong, users can report a listing or user in the app, or email support@unizy.app.

What is a Featured Listing?

A listing the seller has paid to promote. It appears at the top of its category and in Featured Listings on the home screen, with a Featured label. It gets more visibility but doesn’t guarantee a sale. For most categories, the fee is only taken from the seller’s payout if it sells. For Home Search, it’s paid when the listing goes up.

What is Stripe onboarding?

Stripe is the payment company UniZy uses to process payments and pay sellers. Sellers can post listings straight away, but they need to complete Stripe onboarding before they can complete a sale. Stripe asks for details such as a bank account and address. Buyers don’t need to do it.
